Local Stack Connector

A personal, single-user data connector. It is not a public product or service.

About this app

Local Stack Connector is a personal software project. It reads its owner's own Google Drive files and Gmail messages, using read-only access, and copies that content into a knowledge base running entirely on the owner's own computer.

It has one user: the person who built it. It is not distributed, offered to the public, or used by any organisation or third party.

What it accesses

Google Drive content, read-only (drive.readonly), and Gmail messages, read-only (gmail.readonly). The app cannot send email, modify or delete files, or take any other action on the connected Google account.
